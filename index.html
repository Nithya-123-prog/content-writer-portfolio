How I work

A thoughtful process, every time.

Good content starts before the first sentence. Here’s the simple framework I use to keep work relevant, readable and on brief.

01

Understand

Clarify the audience, brief, goal, tone and desired action.

02

Research

Review credible sources, competitors, keywords and search intent.

03

Write

Build a clear structure and write in a natural, audience-first voice.

04

Refine

Check clarity, accuracy, grammar, SEO basics and consistency with the brief.
